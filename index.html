What is the BanJoey

A Cajon, a bass pedal, a cymbal, and a banjo shell walked into a jam session.

The result is the BanJoey—a handmade fusion instrument I designed and built myself. It packs the warmth of a Cajon, the punch of a kick drum, the shimmer of a ride, and the resonance of a banjo shell into one portable, ridiculous, beautiful little kit. I can carry the whole thing to a gig by myself. That's the point.

I've been a drummer my whole life, since my mom caught a five-year-old me trying to hold down a groove on my dad's kit in 1964. After decades of playing, I wanted something that sounded like nothing else. So I built it.

Create

The BanJoey started with one question: what if a drum kit could fit in the back of a car? If that question sounds familiar, you might be a BanJoey person.

Learn

Every instrument teaches you something. This one taught me that constraints are where invention lives. One weird idea, built by hand, led to a sound nobody had heard before.

About Joey

I've been a drummer since before I could reach the kick pedal.

My name is Joey Interrante. I grew up in a house full of music—my dad on guitar, my mom on whatever needed playing. I've been behind a kit since 1964, which means I've had a long time to think about what drums can and can't do. The BanJoey is my answer to all the things they can't.

I built the first one by hand. I play every show on my own, or with whoever shows up. I record, teach, and talk to anyone who wants to talk about music, instruments, or the weird idea they've been sitting on for years. That's what this site is for.
